Frequently Asked Questions

Why do strong passwords matter?

Strong passwords are your first line of defense against unauthorized access to your accounts. Short or predictable passwords can be cracked in seconds by automated tools. A long password mixing uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols makes brute-force attacks computationally infeasible, protecting your email, banking, and personal data from account takeover and identity theft.

Are the generated passwords truly random?

Yes. This tool uses your browser's cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues), not the predictable Math.random function. Every password is generated with real entropy suitable for security purposes.

Is my password stored or transmitted anywhere?

No. Passwords are generated locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript. They are never sent to a server, logged, or stored. Once you leave or refresh the page, the password is gone — be sure to copy it somewhere safe before navigating away.

How long should my password be?

Security experts generally recommend at least 12–16 characters for important accounts. Longer is better: a 20-character random password provides far more security than a short complex one. Never reuse passwords across different sites.
