Frequently Asked Questions

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated by dividing your total word count by 200 words per minute, which is the average silent reading speed for adults. For example, a 1,000-word article is estimated at about 5 minutes to read. This is an approximation — your actual speed may vary depending on the complexity of the text.

Is my text stored or sent anywhere?

No. The Winarty word counter runs entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Your text is never uploaded to a server, stored, or shared with third parties.

What counts as a 'word' or 'character'?

A word is any sequence of characters separated by whitespace. We also report character counts with and without spaces, so you can check limits for social media posts, essays, or SEO descriptions.
