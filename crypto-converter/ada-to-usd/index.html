Frequently Asked Questions

What is Cardano (ADA)?

Cardano is a third-generation blockchain platform founded by Charles Hoskinson, one of Ethereum's co-founders. It uses a peer-reviewed, evidence-based development approach and operates on a proof-of-stake consensus mechanism called Ouroboros, designed for sustainability and security.

What makes Cardano unique?

Cardano distinguishes itself through its rigorous academic approach to development, with protocols peer-reviewed by researchers and scientists. Its layered architecture separates the settlement layer from the computation layer, enabling better scalability and supporting smart contracts through the Plutus programming language.
